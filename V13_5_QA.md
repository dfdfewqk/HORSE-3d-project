# HORSE RANCH V13.5 — Hind Stifle/Hock Airborne Fold (2026-10-16)

Base production Git commit: `b1fbb006566d640aa169388658fcdf995571650f` (V13.4).

## Problem and supporting research
User screenshot showed a rear limb remaining almost straight during gallop recovery. V13.4 retains V13.2 Gallop rotation data; in its 160 sampled steps right Hock moves approx 4–21.5 degrees, while Stifle 12.8–52.1 degrees, and during late swing near frame 120 both are about 4 and 47 degrees, respectively. These are local-rig X rotations, not measured anatomical absolute angles.

References:
- Molenaar (1983), reciprocal apparatus: coupled but not congruous stifle/hock motion: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0264.1983.tb01019.x
- Back et al. (1995), swing phase stifle/tarsal coordination recorded in trot: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7774545/
- Clayton/Hodson (2001), hind-limb kinematics **walking**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11191608/
- Symons et al. (2014), galloping Thoroughbred distal hindlimb kinematics (stance and surface-dependent fetlock behavior): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23742040/

No reference video was directly measured frame by frame: test amplitudes and timings are tuning settings for this 19-bone preview, **not actual biomechanical measurements**.

## Implemented
Add `gallop-v13-5-fold.js` as the preferred Gallop. Preserves all existing animation clips (including V13.2 original), mesh, V13.4 weight correction, 19-bone hierarchy, and bind matrices. Modifies only 4 animation tracks: left/right Hind_Stifle and Hind_Hock rotation. Existing Hip/Fetlock rotation and all other channels are identical references.

At centers RH sample 111, LH 132, the periodic swing envelope (radius 11 to 38 samples) retimes neither footfall schedule nor clip length, but modifies *local rig rotations* by at most -10° Stifle and -18° Hock, gating by original stifle flexion (15°–33°). All values interpolate across 160 samples over original 0.72s cycle. Outside gated swing region, V13.2 angles are unchanged. These are preview model-specific signs, selected based on the actual 3D mesh orientation.
