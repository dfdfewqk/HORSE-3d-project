# HORSE RANCH V13.8 — Whole-body balance experiment (2026-10-16)

Status: EXPERIMENT ONLY. Do NOT merge or deploy. Branch based on V13.7b commit 391a3d0e240c26e30429bb09dc8884f77f918b9f. Keep production main at V13.5 d573079786b29d70d39a5ab52141e47885a33561.

## Actual implementation
New gallop-v13-8.js creates Gallop_V13_8_WHOLE_BODY_BALANCE_CANDIDATE from the existing Gallop_V13_7_INTEGRATED_CANDIDATE, retaining all 14 complete limb-chain tracks (8 hind + 6 fore) and adding five channel modifications: Torso translation, Torso rotation, Neck rotation, Head rotation, Tail rotation. Original animations, rig, skin/weights, bind matrices, GLB and other files are untouched. There is no independent pelvis or hoof bone.
Periodic small local response keys are related to hind/fore distal skin-vertex height proxy minima (RH11, LH35, RF59, LF82 of 160), with a proposed flight-like interval. These are authoring cues, NOT measured contact timings or biomechanical ground reaction profiles.
Local synthetic test maximum added vertical motion is about 0.015 GLB units; added torso pitch magnitude 0.422 degrees; neck 0.284 degrees, head 0.204 degrees, tail 0.400 degrees. These are local small-angle additions, not measured horse angles. Head/neck/tail sample times are preserved even if not on the 160-sample grid. Quaternion multiplication preserves original non-X rotational components.
